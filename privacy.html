Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What we store

When you play Leagledle in Discord and authorize it, we store:

  • your Discord user ID, display name and avatar
  • your game progress: the mode, day, number of guesses, whether you finished, points and timestamps
  • the Discord channel and server where you played, so leaderboards, the “is playing” card and daily recaps can be shown to that group

We only ask Discord for the identify permission. We don’t read your messages, email address, friends list or anything else.

How it’s used

Only to run the game: live progress, leaderboards, streaks and recap messages in the channel where you played. Your name, avatar and scores are visible to other people in that channel or server.

Where it’s kept

Data is stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1). Puzzle progress is also saved in your browser’s local storage. We don’t sell or share your data, and there are no ads or trackers.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, open an issue at github.com/zeadolag/champ-guess with your Discord username, and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
